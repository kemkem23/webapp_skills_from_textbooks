# PR/FAQ Quality Checklist (from *Working Backwards*)

Use this checklist before finalizing any PR/FAQ.

## Customer Obsession

- The primary customer is explicitly named.
- The customer pain is concrete and urgent, not generic.
- The proposed value is described from customer perspective.

## Press Release Quality

- Headline states a clear customer outcome.
- First paragraph answers who/what/why now.
- Body explains how experience changes for customers.
- Quotes are believable and specific (no hype words).

## FAQ Quality

- External FAQs address adoption, pricing, trust, and limitations.
- Internal FAQs address architecture, operations, legal/compliance, and support.
- Every answer includes a concrete decision, metric, or policy.

## Decision Readiness

- 3–5 leading metrics and lagging metrics are defined.
- Major risks and mitigation owners are listed.
- Explicit go/no-go criteria are present.
- Open assumptions are tagged with validation steps and due dates.
